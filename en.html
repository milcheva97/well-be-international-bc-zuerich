Club Codex

Trust needs clear principles.

The codex is an integral part of membership and is accepted upon joining.

Confidentiality

The Chatham House Rule applies: content may be used, but individuals and specific attributions must not be disclosed externally.

No hard selling

The club is not a sales forum. Presentations and contributions serve substantive value—not pitching.

Fair dialogue

Speaking time is shared fairly. The moderator intervenes when necessary to avoid dominance or monologues.

Conflicts of interest

Potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Members maintain a respectful tone even where interests differ.

Participation expectation

Members are expected to participate actively, with a goal of attending at least 60–70% of events each year.

Breaches of the codex

For repeated or serious breaches, club management may issue a warning or exclude a member without refund of fees already paid.
